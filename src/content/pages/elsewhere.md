A small index of things from the rest of the internet that have survived
several clear-outs.

- [A list of dice problems](https://www.madandmoonly.com/doctormatt/misc.htm) — of interest to the aspiring probabilist.
- [Detexify](https://detexify.kirelabs.org/classify.html) — draw a symbol, learn its name. Artificial intelligence applied to the one problem that deserved it.
- [Convert](https://p2r3.github.io/convert/) — converts file A into file B.
- [quiver](https://q.uiver.app/) — a commutative diagram editor, with one-click export to LaTeX.
- [Firefox](https://www.firefox.com/) — still.
