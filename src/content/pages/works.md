Programs, scripts and small curiosities. There is a recurring fondness here
for problems that sit just on the edge of being useful.

## polygonal approximator · python, 2024

An evolution-based program that converts images into the vertices of polygons,
or the foci of ellipses, badly. Output was fed to
[Desmos](https://www.desmos.com/3d/hdifqltoov) for rendering, which was never
the plan and became the best part.

## quiet tools · bash and python, ongoing

A collection of scripts written for the parsing of large databases and the
automation of indignities. Most move data from where it is to where I would
prefer it. Several were used once and never again, which was the correct
number of times.

## a typeset desktop · hyprland, 2026

Configuration for a lock screen, and then a widget that turns the desktop into
a tastefully set information hub. I am told this is all the rage amongst the
youth.

## this · astro, 2026

Set entirely in Computer Modern: the typewriter cut for the frame, the roman
for prose. Every rule on the page is the character `─` rather than a border,
which means the lines are typeset alongside the text instead of drawn around
it. Nobody asked for this.
